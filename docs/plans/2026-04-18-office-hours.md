## The 6 forcing questions

### 1) Who is the user with a *desperate* need?
Draft hypothesis (to confirm):
- Adults (25–55) with chronic musculoskeletal pain (back/neck/shoulder), who’ve tried physio/meds and still have frequent flare-ups.
- They are functional enough to use an app, but distressed and stuck.

**Pick ONE to start:**
- Option A: chronic back/neck pain office workers
- Option B: postpartum pain / pelvic pain
- Option C: migraine sufferers
- Option D: arthritis / older adults

### 2) What are they doing today (the status quo)?
Draft status quo:
- YouTube stretches, generic mindfulness apps, inconsistent physio exercises.
- Painkiller use during flare-ups.
- Occasional doctor visits; low adherence to plans.

### 3) What makes them act *now* (trigger)?
Draft triggers:
- A flare-up that threatens work/sleep.
- A new diagnosis or scary scan.
- A recommendation from a therapist/doctor.

### 4) What’s the narrowest wedge we can ship that’s still valuable?
Draft wedge:
- A **10-minute “flare-up protocol”** that produces a *personalized, safe* plan:
  - quick check-in (pain location, severity, red flags, current constraints)
  - a short sequence (breath + micro-movement + cognitive reframe)
  - clear “stop / seek care” rules
  - follow-up: “what changed after 10 minutes?”

### 5) What do we need to *observe* to know it’s working?
Draft observable signals (process metrics):
- Session completion rate
- Self-reported pain change (0–10 before vs after)
- Sleep impact next day (simple 1 question)
- Repeat use within 7 days

### 6) Why are we the ones who can win (future-fit / unfair advantage)?
Draft advantage:
- Therapist-led content + protocols designed for adherence.
- Strong safety boundaries + escalation logic.
- Focused wedge (pain flare-up) rather than broad “mental wellness”.

## Proposed MVP (thin vertical slice)
**Flow:**
1) User answers 6–10 intake questions (including red flags).
2) System outputs a structured “next 10 minutes” plan + safety disclaimers.
3) User records immediate outcome (better/same/worse + pain 0–10).

**No persistence by default** until we decide consent + retention.
